Executive Summary BUD

Grupo Consultores de Comunicacion Integrada y Estrategia Digital SL

Grupo Consultores is one of the leading independent communications consultancies in Spain, with over two decades of experience in the sector. The firm has established itself as a reference point for the advertising, communications, and media industry through its comprehensive consulting services, industry benchmarking studies, and agency evaluation methodologies.

The company operates across multiple service lines including strategic communications consulting, agency search and selection processes, media auditing, and industry research. Their annual studies on the Spanish advertising market are widely regarded as the definitive benchmark for the sector, covering agency performance, client satisfaction, and industry trends.

With a team of experienced consultants and a proprietary methodology developed over 20 years, the company serves both advertisers and agencies. Their unique positioning as an independent evaluator of the advertising ecosystem gives them access to privileged market intelligence that is difficult to replicate by competitors.

Company Overview
Company Overview BUD

Founded in the early 2000s, Grupo Consultores has grown from a small boutique consultancy into one of the most influential firms in the Spanish communications industry. The company's evolution mirrors the transformation of the advertising sector itself, having adapted its service offering to encompass digital communications, programmatic media, influencer marketing evaluation, and ESG communications consulting.

The firm's business model is built on three pillars: consulting services for advertisers seeking to optimize their agency relationships, research and benchmarking studies that provide industry-wide intelligence, and training programs for communications professionals. This diversified revenue model provides stability and recurring income streams.

Market Position

Grupo Consultores occupies a unique and defensible position in the Spanish communications market. As the primary independent evaluator of advertising agencies and communications firms, the company benefits from network effects: the more agencies and advertisers participate in their studies, the more valuable the benchmarking data becomes for all participants. This creates a virtuous cycle that is extremely difficult for new entrants to disrupt.

The company's competitive moat is further strengthened by its 20+ year database of industry performance metrics, which provides historical trend analysis that cannot be replicated by newcomers. Their annual SCOPEN study is considered the gold standard for agency evaluation in Spain and increasingly in Latin American markets.
